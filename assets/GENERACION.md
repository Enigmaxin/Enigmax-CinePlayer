# Identidad elegida

Logo completo: opción A original, aprobada por el usuario. Se conserva la imagen original sin cambios en logo-opcion-A.png. No se ha utilizado la propuesta combinada A+D.

Herramienta: generación de imágenes integrada. La adaptación del símbolo se ha generado tomando el logo A como referencia, para eliminar texto y fondo y obtener un PNG con transparencia. El ICO es una conversión técnica de ese PNG a nueve resoluciones de Windows mediante Pillow; no redibuja el diseño.

Prompt de adaptación exacto:

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: Windows application icon derived from the user's approved logo, OPTION A. EDIT the reference by extracting and centering ONLY its main large upper emblem: the graceful curved gold 35mm filmstrip wrapping around the blue right-pointing PLAY triangle. Preserve the actual recognizable emblem design, the curving gold ribbon silhouette and its film perforations, blue triangular center, gold border, and refined cinematic finish. Remove ALL lettering and the small duplicate emblem at the bottom. Do not include frames, stereo rectangles, new initials or any other concepts from other proposals. One centered emblem only, compact, large on a square canvas with a comfortable 8% clear margin on all sides, no clipped extremities. Transparent background with real alpha. Remove the far-reaching light rays and the atmospheric background glow, retaining crisp emblem edges and gentle highlights within the emblem. No background plate, no mockup. This is a functional icon adaptation of the selected design, not a new design exploration.
